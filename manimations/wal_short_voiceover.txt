00.0-03.2  Your database says commit.
03.2-06.4  Then the power dies before the data reaches disk.
06.4-08.7  So why isn't the transaction lost?
08.7-14.4  Before acknowledging the commit, the database writes the change to a durable write-ahead log.
14.4-17.7  After a crash, it replays that log and restores the data.
17.7-20.0  Record first. Apply later.
