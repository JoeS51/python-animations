00.0-03.5  Your database says commit.
03.5-06.5  Then the power dies before the change reaches the data file.
06.5-09.0  So why isn't the transaction lost?
09.0-14.8  Before acknowledging commit, the database writes the change to a durable write-ahead log.
14.8-17.8  After a crash, it replays that log and restores the data.
17.8-20.0  Record first. Apply later.
